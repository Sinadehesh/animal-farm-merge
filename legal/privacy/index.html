Privacy Policy

Merge Farmstead · last updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Merge Farmstead ("the game", "we") uses when you play it on Android or in a web browser, and why. In short: the game keeps your progress on your own device, does not ask you for personal information, and does not sell or share data about you.

Information the game stores

  • Your game progress (your farm, items, coins, gems, levels and settings) is saved on your device only. It is not sent to us.
  • The game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.
  • The game does not use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.

In-app purchases

Purchases in the Android app are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never see or store your card or payment details. When a purchase succeeds, Google Play tells the game which item was bought and gives a transaction reference, which the game uses only to deliver the item and to make sure it is delivered once. Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The game does not currently show ads from any ad network. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it happens, describing the ad provider and what it collects.

Internet use

The Android app contains everything it needs to play and works offline; it connects to Google Play only for purchases. The web version loads the game from its website and may keep a copy of the game's files in your browser so it can open offline. Like any website, the host's servers may record standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) in their logs; we do not use this to identify you.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you control it: uninstalling the app, clearing the app's storage, or clearing your browser's site data for the game deletes it permanently. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete those emails.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game or its store listing.
